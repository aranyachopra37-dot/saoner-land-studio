  • 2. Switching Between 3D Orbit & 2D Blueprint:
    • Click 📐 2D Blueprint at the top of the canvas to see a flat top-down CAD layout with exact meter dimensions (`100m × 56.58m`), North-Up orientation, and compass rose.

  • 3. Modifying Father's Civil BOQ & Resetting to Standard:
    • In Section 5 on the right panel, you can type directly into any green cost box (`Brick Wall`, `9m Road & Drain`, `MSEB`, `Villas`) to test your own contractor/item-rate estimates.
    • Click ↺ Reset BOQ to Standard (or ↺ Reset All to Standard in the top bar) at any time to restore Hermes's recommended baseline.
  • 4. Exporting to AutoCAD / Sharing Online:
    • Click 📐 Export CAD (.DXF) to download a `.dxf` file of your current slider settings for AutoCAD / Civil 3D.
