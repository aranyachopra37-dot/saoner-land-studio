How to use this studio

  1. Look: "3D orbit": drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, Shift-drag or right-drag to pan. "2D CAD plan" is north-up with true dimensions, bearings, chainages and plot areas. Hover over a plot for its details.
  2. Check the boundary: In 2D, switch on "Satellite". The red boundary should sit on the field edges in the satellite image. If it doesn't, tell us; the corner coordinates are in the Engineering tab.
  3. Change the plan: The sliders on the left move the gate, road width, plot frontage, row depth and open space %. Everything (plots, BOQ, money) recalculates immediately.
  4. Your own rates: In the BOQ tab, every green box is an item rate. Type your contractor rate and the totals update. "Reset rates" brings back the defaults.
  5. Money: The Pro-forma tab has land cost, approvals, plot rate, sales speed and tax. The chart shows month-by-month cash.
  6. Share / CAD: "Copy share link" saves your exact settings in the URL. "Export DXF" downloads the current layout for AutoCAD / Civil 3D (metres, true north).

This is a concept layout drawn from a Google Maps trace (about ±1.5 m). Do not set out on site from it. That needs the TILR measurement plan and a level survey.
